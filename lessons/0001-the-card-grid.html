Build your first card grid

One goal, about 10 minutes: arrange eight cards into four columns and two rows, as in your Riftbound collection.

1. Separate structure from appearance

HTML gives the page its elements. CSS controls their presentation. This parent contains eight children:

<div class="card-grid">
  <div>Card 1</div>
  <div>Card 2</div>
  <!-- Continue through Card 8. -->
</div>

Apply layout rules to the parent. Its direct children become grid items when you give it display: grid. Source: MDN.

2. Read one example

.card-grid {
  display: grid;
  grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr);
  gap: 16px;
}

repeat(3, 1fr) makes three equal columns. 1fr is one share of available grid space. gap separates the rows and columns. Source: MDN.

3. Write it yourself

Before typing, predict: how many rows would eight cards need in three columns?
